        How to play

        The goal of a hand

        Beat the dealer by getting a hand total closer to 21 without going over. Number cards count their number, J/Q/K count 10, an Ace counts 11 or 1 (whichever is better).

        Winning the match

        Everyone starts with the same chips. The first player to reach the goal (shown at the top, 10× the starting chips by default) wins. If nobody gets there, the last player with chips wins. Players who run out of chips are out; the longer you survive, the higher you rank. No rebuys.

        Each round

        1. Bet. Tap chips, then Place bet. You have 25 seconds; minimum bet is $10.
        2. Deal. Everyone gets two cards, the dealer gets one up and one face down.
        3. Play. Take turns in seat order. When it is your turn, choose an action.
        4. Dealer. Flips the hidden card and draws until reaching 17 or more. The dealer stands on all 17s.

        Your actions

        • Hit (H): take another card. Over 21 is a bust and you lose the bet.
        • Stand (S): keep your total.
        • Double (D): double your bet, take exactly one more card, then stand. Only on your first two cards.
        • Split (P): if your first two cards have the same value, play them as two hands. It costs a second bet. Split Aces get one card each. One split per round.
        • Insurance: when the dealer shows an Ace you may bet half your bet that the dealer has Blackjack. It pays 2 to 1.

        Payouts

        • Win: 1 to 1 · Blackjack (Ace + 10-card on your first two cards): 3 to 2 · Push (tie): bet returned.
        • A 21 after splitting counts as a normal 21, not a Blackjack.

        Good to know

        If you do not act in time you stand automatically. If you do not bet in time your last bet is placed. Your hand total is always shown under your cards.
